Welcome to Circus Land

Children's playland located inside Landmark Mall in Doha, Qatar. It is a whimsical, colorful space with circus-themed rides and games offers children a place to play, explore, discover, and create wonderful memories. Our park features a range of attractions and entertainment options suitable for children, including exciting Arcade and Redemption Games, Fun Rides, Soft Play Areas, Trampolines, and Kidz Cinema.
